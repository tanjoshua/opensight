FAQ

Fair questions

Straight answers about what OpenSight does — and doesn't.

Is this exactly what a customer sees in their ChatGPT app?

Very close, and honest about the difference: we query ChatGPT's models through OpenAI's official API with web search enabled — the same engine, without any one user's personal chat history influencing the result. That makes it a fair, repeatable measure of how ChatGPT treats your business. We record the exact model with every single run, so when models change, your trends stay interpretable.

Which AI platforms do you monitor?

ChatGPT. It's where the overwhelming share of customer AI questions happen today, so that's what OpenSight measures — properly, with full raw responses stored.

Does OpenSight help me improve, or just measure?

Both — measurement is what makes improvement possible. The dashboard shows which questions you're missing from, which sources ChatGPT trusts for your market, how it describes you, and who wins the questions you lose. That's a concrete to-do list: where to earn a listing or review, what to fix in your website's story, which competitor to study. Act on it, and next week's run measures whether it moved the needle. What OpenSight won't do is hand you generic advice — every insight comes from your actual answers.

Do I need to install anything or change my website?

No. OpenSight works entirely from the outside, the same way a customer does. Setup is your business name and website address.

Can I change the 20 questions?

Yes — edit or replace any of them whenever you like. When you replace a question, its old history is kept and the new one starts a fresh trend, so your data stays honest. We'll warn you before anything is discarded — nothing ever is silently.

How fast do I see something useful?

Same day. Your first monitoring run starts the moment you approve your questions — you don't wait for next week's cycle to see your first results.

What kinds of businesses does this work for?

Any local or specialist service business that customers find by asking for a recommendation — clinics, home services, studios, professional practices. Your 20 questions are generated from your website for your trade.
