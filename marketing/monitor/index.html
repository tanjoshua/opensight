Where it starts

The right questions, not a keyword list.

Nobody types “endodontist singapore” into ChatGPT. They describe a problem and ask who to see.

OpenSight reads your website and drafts 20 questions in that shape — your services, your area, the way a customer would put it. You review and edit every one before the first run, and can replace them later without losing the history you already have.

A monitored answer

The whole answer, not a mention count.

Every run stores the question, the model, the run time, the full response, every business named in it, the order they were named in, and every citation.

That is what makes the trend interpretable months later: when a number moves, you open the answers underneath it and read what changed. Models change too, so the exact one is recorded with every run.
