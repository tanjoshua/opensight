Why it matters

Your next customer isn't Googling. They're asking ChatGPT.

"Who's a reliable aircon company near me?" used to be a Google search. Now it's one of the 2.5 billion prompts ChatGPT answers every day — and what comes back is a paragraph, not a results page.
